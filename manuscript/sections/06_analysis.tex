\section{Analysis and Future Directions}\label{sec:analysis}


\paragraph{Reference frames determine whether an intervention succeeds.}

CAD favors source adherence; Astute RAG reconciles knowledge conflicts, while Stable-RAG addresses passage-order sensitivity \citep{shi-etal-2024-trusting,wang-etal-2025-astute,zhang-etal-2026-stable}. These mechanisms target different failures: following an incorrect source can improve faithfulness without improving world correctness. We therefore propose varying evidence correctness and order with questions and the generator fixed, scoring source support and world correctness separately and retaining unknown labels. This tests sensitivity to supplied evidence. Attributing errors to retrieval selection additionally requires auditing retrieved passages separately from the generator's use of them.

\paragraph{Confidence and internal signals are useful proxies, not truth certificates.}

Semantic entropy targets unstable confabulations \citep{extsemanticentropy2024}, whereas \citet{cheang-etal-2026-llms} find that associated hallucinations resemble factual recall in the representations studied. These findings suggest that uncertainty and probing may detect missing knowledge more readily than confidently recalled misinformation, without establishing a universal detector ranking. We propose testing held-out generators, separating unstable answers from consistent falsehoods and checking whether external verification helps on the latter. Aggregate accuracy cannot identify which failure process a detector recognizes.

\paragraph{Metric improvements must preserve useful information.}

Response length affects measured precision \citep{zhao-etal-2025-response}, while ICAT evaluates coverage of expected aspects \citep{samarinas-etal-2025-beyond}. Precision assesses selected content; coverage assesses whether required content is present. We recommend reporting both, unique supported claims, and refusals, with risk compared at matched answered-question fractions. Revision also needs counts of corrections, new errors, and retained supported claims; matched-length analysis tests whether gains mainly reflect less information.

\paragraph{Evaluation must remain independent of optimization.}
\citet{godbole-jia-2025-verify} show that similar evaluator accuracy can conceal different class-specific errors and biased system-level estimates. Using the same verifier for optimization and evaluation therefore risks rewarding its preferences. We recommend independent auditing across domains and generators. Temporal benchmark errors \citep{jiang-etal-2026-benchmarks} additionally motivate versioned evidence and label review. Interventions in Figure~\ref{fig:mitigation} require independent evaluation against Figure~\ref{fig:evaluation}'s targets and Table~\ref{tab:hallucination-benchmarks}'s benchmarks under matched evidence, coverage, and budgets.

\paragraph{Resource trade-offs depend on the intervention stage.}
FactAlign pays for factuality feedback and parameter updates; Self-RAG combines offline supervision with online retrieval and candidate selection \citep{huang-chen-2024-factalign,extselfrag2024}. CoVe and CRITIC instead add verification and revision calls during use \citep{dhuliawala-etal-2024-chain,extcritic2024}. Training-free operation can still be costly; training expenditure may be amortized across queries but repeated after domain changes. Report both stages, including tokens, requests, latency, and memory.

\paragraph{Detection and mitigation require joint validation.}
An intervention can improve generation while making remaining errors harder to detect. \citet{cheng-etal-2025-chain} find that reasoning prompts can reduce hallucinations while obscuring detection cues in their studied settings. For a fixed query distribution, let $E$ denote an erroneous candidate and $R$ the decision to release it. For $P(E)>0$, the erroneous-release rate satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:release}
P(E\cap R)=P(E)P(R\mid E).
\end{equation}
No independence is assumed. Lower candidate error can be offset by higher false acceptance, so component improvements need not improve the complete system.

We propose comparing a baseline, individual interventions, and their combination under matched questions, evidence, and budgets, crossing generators with fixed detectors. Report erroneous releases per query, error among released answers, and coverage; refusals remain unreleased. Audit corrections, new errors, and retained supported claims at matched coverage to distinguish correction from reduced answering. Appendix~\ref{app:protocol} details this proposed protocol.

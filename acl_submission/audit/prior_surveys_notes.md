# Prior-survey positioning and bounded update

Checked on 5 October 2026. This is a targeted comparison supporting the present revision, not an exhaustive systematic search or a claim of priority. It supplements the fixed thirteen-study audit defined in `codebook.md`; contextual papers do not enter that audit's denominator. The old eighty-seven-item coursework library is not a target quota.

## Comparison basis

`prior_surveys.csv` contains five close surveys, positive descriptions of their coverage, exact full-text locators, and a defensible incremental role for the present audit. The comparison used relevant full-text definitions, evaluation, mitigation, synthesis, and protocol sections. It does not infer omissions from abstracts, nor claim that every page was read to equal depth.

The established background already includes source/world distinctions, generation-versus-discrimination evaluation, pipeline decomposition, heterogeneous protocol concerns, information retention, cost trade-offs, and concerns about shared detector or evaluator failure modes. A new taxonomy diagram or a more recent bibliography alone would therefore be an insufficient positioning argument.

The proposed increment is the **explicit unit and evidence product**: a bounded, reproducible documentary audit of thirteen representative method/evaluation configurations. Its fields connect the reference frame and scoring unit to information preservation, evaluator separation, resource reporting, and generator/intervention/detector validation. The contribution should be demonstrated through the completed evidence ledger and specific case comparisons, rather than asserted as the first treatment of these issues.

The closest reporting precedent is [Schmidtova et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2025.inlg-main.4/), whose human-evaluation audit requires explicit acknowledgement. Our denominator concerns representative automatic detection and mitigation studies, not an estimate of field-wide reporting prevalence. A different unit does not itself establish novelty; the added value depends on the resulting actionable comparisons.

## Suggested related-work paragraph

```latex
\citet{Ji_2023} distinguish source faithfulness from world factuality across NLG tasks, while \citet{wang-etal-2024-factuality} organize LLM factuality evaluation and enhancement and explicitly identify obstacles to fair comparison. \citet{zhang-etal-2025-sirens} and \citet{Alansari_2026} further analyze benchmarks, mitigation stages, evaluator limitations, and task-dependent trade-offs. \citet{schmidtova-etal-2025-eyes} audit human-evaluation reporting, including definitions, annotation procedures, and agreement. Building on these perspectives, our bounded audit examines how thirteen representative studies document the conditions needed to compare or combine detection and mitigation: information preservation, evaluator separation, resources, and joint validation.
```

Use “examines” or “provides” once the extraction and audit are complete. Do not replace this with “first,” “comprehensive coverage of all work,” or “prior surveys only list methods.” Keep the section numbering required by the frozen Figure 2.

## Appropriate contribution and result boundaries

1. **Compatibility synthesis:** make explicit which target, evidence, scoring unit, and access assumptions must agree before a cross-study comparison is meaningful. Credit the prior conceptual discussions rather than recasting them as new discoveries.
2. **Traceable documentary evidence:** release the predefined sample, codebook, primary-source versions, locators, extracted descriptions, and unresolved cases. State that extraction is AI-assisted, with final author verification required; neither agent review nor a second model constitutes independent human annotation.
3. **Prospective validation agenda:** link the documentary findings to proposed matched-coverage and combined-system checks. These are experiments to perform, not results from this survey. The erroneous-release factorization is a probability identity and an organizing device, not a new theorem or proof that a particular combination fails.

Avoid “missing evaluation” when the code means “not located in inspected sections.” A reported task-quality metric, generated-fact count, or separate judge is not automatically a matched-information control, completeness measure, or statistically independent evaluation. Do not pool heterogeneous benchmark numbers or rank universal cost effectiveness.

## Bounded update findings

The log records actual discovery queries and record-level decisions through 5 October 2026. Primary proceedings, publisher pages, DOI deposits, and arXiv versions were used. The `nature-academic-search` workflow was consulted; its optional academic-search MCP was not available, so direct primary-site search and metadata retrieval were used. Search rankings and complete result sets were not archived; this log must not be represented as a systematic retrieval census.

[PROBE](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2099/) was checked in its official full text, specifically §§2–4, §5.3, and §7 (PDF pp.2–6, 8–9). It decomposes source-faithfulness detection into claim decomposition, evidence finding, evidence evaluation, and localization, with a synthetic Wikipedia-based benchmark. Thus, process-level diagnosis is already an active research contribution. Our documentary audit has a different output and must not claim the first process decomposition. PROBE remains a context record; it is not added to `new_refs.bib` or the thirteen-study denominator. Its paper contains varying aggregate claim counts, so this revision should not repeat those counts without a dedicated reconciliation.

Two further 2026 primary records were screened only at metadata/abstract level: a broader September review on [detection, verification, and correction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlp.2026.100231), and a domain-specific [medical detector/intervention study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2026.105081). They reinforce the need for modest positioning. They were not used to assert full-text coverage or gaps, were not added to the bounded full-text survey comparison, and do not justify expanding a pre-fixed audit sample after observing results.

[HalluScoring 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38355), dated 29 September 2026, was screened as an Arabic shared-task update. Its metadata/abstract is outside the English-focused audit and supplies no absence or performance claim in the manuscript. The bounded search supports a new analytical lens, not an exhaustive “latest survey” assertion.

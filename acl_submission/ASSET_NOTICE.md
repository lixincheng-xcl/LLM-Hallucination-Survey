# Asset provenance and scope

The evidence records contain original paraphrased descriptions of public research, with links, version digests, and passage locators. Their intended use is documentary research and verification; they are not a benchmark or deployment certification. Public author names appear only as bibliographic attribution. No participant dataset or model weights are redistributed.

Full source papers are not included. Their publisher or repository terms continue to apply; a public URL is not treated as a blanket redistribution license. Existing ACL style files retain their original notices. Figure layouts follow the two surveys cited in Appendix A; content is adapted to this survey and visual provenance remains credited. The supplied figure PDFs are preserved unchanged.

The author must confirm release licensing for newly written manuscript text, diagrams, audit records, and scripts before any blanket reuse license is declared. No license for third-party source papers is implied by this package.

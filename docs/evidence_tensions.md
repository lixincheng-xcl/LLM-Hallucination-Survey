# Evidence tensions and future-research questions

These are preparation notes for critical synthesis, not manuscript prose or established universal conclusions. Claims remain bounded by each study's models, tasks and evaluation protocol.

| Tension | Evidence to compare | Technical explanation to examine | Testable future direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truth versus source support | [Hallucinated but Factual](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.236/); [Correctness and Faithfulness](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.38/); [HalluLens](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1176/) | Studies change the reference frame: world, supplied source, or training-data scope. Identical labels can refer to different targets. | Annotate world correctness and source support separately, retaining an unknown/unverifiable state. |
| Internal knowledge versus truth | [Internal-state probe](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.68/); [Truthfulness hyperplane](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1012/); [Hidden-state limits](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.304/); [Recall vs truthfulness](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.34/) | Synthetic labels, model-dependent generation, dataset diversity and associated versus unassociated errors change what a probe can learn. | Evaluate a fixed probe on matched natural-error sets, with knowledge-recall and correctness labels separated. |
| Consistency versus correctness | [SelfCheckGPT](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.557/); [SAC3](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.1032/); [Semantic entropy](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07421-0) | Repeated samples or multiple models can agree on the same mistaken association. Meaning-level uncertainty targets a particular failure mechanism. | Combine consistency with independent retrieved evidence and report performance specifically on consistent falsehoods. |
| More retrieval versus better evidence | [CAD](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-short.69/); [Astute RAG](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1476/); [Stable-RAG](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1188/); [Source preferences](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1357/) | Stronger context use can help accurate context but amplify conflicting, repeated or unreliable evidence; document order also matters. | Factorially vary evidence truth, repetition, source identity and order while holding the question and generator fixed. |
| High precision versus useful answers | [FActScore](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/); [Core](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1018/); [ICAT](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.693/); [Response-length bias](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.161/) | Shortening, repetition and selection of easy claims can improve precision without satisfying the information need. | Report factual precision alongside unique informative claims, aspect coverage, refusal rate and matched-length evaluations. |
| Better score versus better detector | [AggreFact](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.650/); [FaithBench](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-short.38/); [Verify with Caution](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1175/); [TRIVIA+](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.680/) | Old generators, artificial negatives, judge bias, domain shift and label noise create different apparent rankings. | Evaluate under held-out generator families, natural errors and explicitly modeled noisy labels, with human auditing. |
| Better reasoning versus detectable uncertainty | [CoT obscures cues](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.67/); [Hallucination tax](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.112/); [Benchmark aging](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-short.37/) | Prompting, reinforcement tuning and time change score distributions, refusal behavior and even the reference labels. The math-heavy RFT study is supporting evidence, not a general proof about factual QA. | Recalibrate detectors across generator updates and timestamps; track risk–coverage curves rather than a single accuracy number. |

Future empirical work would need its own implementation and controlled measurements. None has been run as part of this survey preparation.

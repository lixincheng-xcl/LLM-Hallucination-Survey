# Rights and attribution

The cited publications and their associated software or datasets retain their original rights and licenses. A link in this repository does not relicense a paper or endorse its claims. Source PDFs and full abstracts are not redistributed here.

The original curation notes, diagrams and scripts were prepared for this survey project. No separate open-source license is assigned in this preparation release; public availability is not a blanket grant to redistribute every component. A project license can be selected when the author finalizes the release policy.

Academic reuse of ideas, datasets, figures and tables requires appropriate attribution. The project diagrams synthesize ideas from multiple cited studies and should retain the provenance file when reused. No original study's experimental findings are represented as new experiments conducted by this project.
